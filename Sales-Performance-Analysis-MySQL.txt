## Python Data Analysis

### Sales Performance Analysis using Python

The same sales dataset was analyzed using Python to calculate business KPIs and identify sales trends.

### Libraries Used

* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib

### Analysis Performed

* Total revenue and sales KPIs
* Monthly sales analysis
* Product revenue analysis
* City-wise sales analysis
* Category sales analysis
* Top customer analysis
* Quantity sold by product
* Revenue vs. quantity comparison

### Key Results

* **Total Revenue:** ₹294,400
* **Total Orders:** 10
* **Total Units Sold:** 24
* **Average Order Value:** ₹29,440
* **Top Revenue Product:** Laptop — ₹115,000
* **Top Quantity Product:** Keyboard — 7 units
* **Top Revenue City:** Pune — ₹145,000
* **Top Category:** Electronics — 92.73% of revenue
